words-fr.txt, lemmas-fr.txt and common-fr.txt are derived from Lexique 3.83
(New, Pallier et al., http://www.lexique.org), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: lowercase words of 3-20 letters without hyphens or apostrophes. words-fr.txt keeps every
spelling; lemmas-fr.txt keeps the headwords of nouns, adjectives, verbs and adverbs; common-fr.txt
keeps those headwords with a film or book frequency of at least 1 per million.
These derived files are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
